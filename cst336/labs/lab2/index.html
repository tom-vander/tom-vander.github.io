Guess the Number!

Here's how to play:

A random number has been selected and it's your job to guess what it is.
The number is between 1 and 99 (inclusive).


You are limited to seven guesses:

  • Guess correctly before using them all, you win!
  • Guess incorrectly seven times, you lose!
  • With each incorrect guess, you will know whether your guess was low or high.
  • The number of attempts you've made, as well as previous guesses, will be displayed each match.

Good Luck and Have Fun!
