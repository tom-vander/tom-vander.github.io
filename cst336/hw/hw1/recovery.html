How to recover from malware attack

  1. Assess what devices are infected

    Determine what devices seem to be affected. Consider anything that has been either connected physically, wirelessly, or over network services.

  2. Isolate infected devices

    Disconnect any infected devices from all physical and wireless connections. Be sure to turn off the device if you have any trouble removing these connections.

  3. Identify malware and report

    Once the devices have been isolated, begin attempting to identify any malware. If you cannot identify any and are unsure if you proceed safely, considering investing in technical support services to help you. You will do the same if you cannot find a way to disconnect earlier devices

  4. Restore device backups or factory reset

    Remove malware or apply clean physical or cloud backups to all infected devices. If for any reason these options are unavailable, consider resetting your devices and starting from scratch.
