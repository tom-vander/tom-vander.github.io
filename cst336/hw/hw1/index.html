What Is Malware?

Malware is code or software programmed to maliciously invade a computer or network system.

The scale and impact of malware varies, depending mostly on how well they can handle dealing with security measures and how easily they can travel between systems.

Why Does It Matter To Users?

Malware attacks can leave users susceptible to data leaks, while also being capable of disrupting the performance of personal and professional computer systems.

Attackers can use sensitive user information for financial gain, to fulfill political or personal agendas, or simply to bring themselves amusement.

As advancements are made in CS, malware is becoming less detectable, more capable, and easier to implement.

How Can Users Be Prepared?

Users can prepare themselves to deal with malware by learning what common types exist, the tools that are available to actively defend against attacks, and what actions can be made to recover from a successful attack.

This site is intended to act as an informational tool for users to learn about these dangers, spread awareness, and advocate for higher digital security for all.
