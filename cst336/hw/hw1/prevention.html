How to protect yourself from malware?

  • Keep your devices updated:

    Keeping up with system updates ensures that known security vulnerabilities have been patched and available security improvements have been applied

  • Use security services and tools

    Security services and tools are available to defend your devices, accounts, and other sensitive information, like VPNs, password managers, and multi-factor authentications.

  • Act consciously and cautiously

    Think consciously when operating online, like going through e-mails, opening webpages, or downloading files and programs. Consider risks when connecting unknown devices to your network or other personal devices.
